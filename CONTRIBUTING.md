# Contributing to the Country 196 Public Record

Country 196 welcomes clear questions, verifiable evidence and constructive proposals from people everywhere.

## Before You Contribute

- Address ideas, not identities.
- Explain the real-world problem before proposing technology.
- Separate evidence from personal belief.
- State who benefits, who carries the risk and how success can be measured.
- Do not use nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender or political identity to reduce another person's dignity.

## Contribution Types

### Answer a Founding Question

Give one direct answer, explain why it matters and describe a testable replacement.

### Submit a Proposal

Use this structure:

1. Problem
2. People affected
3. Proposed system
4. Risks and safeguards
5. Real-world test
6. Measurement of success

### Report a Contradiction

If a public claim conflicts with a founding principle or another decision record, identify both statements and explain the contradiction clearly.

## Standard

The goal is not to win an argument. The goal is to help build systems worthy of human trust.
