# Founding Question 001

## What should Country 196 refuse to inherit from the old world?

There are 195 widely recognized countries in the world. Each emerged from a different history, but many carry the same inherited failures into the present.

Country 196 begins by asking what must be left behind before deciding what should be built.

Possible answers include:

- Corruption
- Bureaucracy without purpose
- Inherited privilege
- Political division
- Discrimination by nationality or geography
- Economic systems that reward extraction over contribution
- Algorithmic manipulation without accountability
- Institutions that protect themselves before protecting people

## Your Response

Choose only one inherited failure.

Then answer three questions:

1. Why must it be excluded?
2. What system should replace it?
3. How could that replacement be tested in real life?
