# Community Standard

Country 196 is intended to connect people across borders without reproducing the hostility, exclusion and inherited hierarchy of the old world.

Participants must:

- Protect human dignity.
- Debate systems without attacking people.
- Avoid harassment, threats and dehumanizing language.
- Disclose material conflicts of interest.
- Correct false claims when reliable evidence is provided.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.

Participation may be restricted when conduct repeatedly prevents others from contributing safely and meaningfully.

Freedom of thought is protected. Abuse of people is not.
